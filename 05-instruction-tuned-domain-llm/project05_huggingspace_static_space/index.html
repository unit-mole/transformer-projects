RESPONSIBLE REPORTING

Known limitations and lessons

01

Fine-tuning improved the baseline

Both LoRA experiments improved several automated metrics over untouched FLAN-T5-base.

02

Higher LoRA capacity was not automatically better

Experiment 2 used a higher rank but did not outperform Experiment 1 overall.

03

Automated metrics were insufficient

Human factual review exposed weak answers that similarity metrics could not reliably identify.

04

Negative findings were preserved

Experiment 2 remains documented rather than being hidden after it failed the final release decision.

Model limitation

The adapters may generate incomplete, circular, or factually inaccurate explanations. This project is an educational demonstration of instruction tuning, controlled evaluation, and responsible model selection—not a production-grade expert assistant.
