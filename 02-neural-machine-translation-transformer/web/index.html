Manual error analysis

  • Named entities, numbers, dates, and product terminology
  • Word order, gender, tense, and agreement
  • Under-translation and over-translation
  • Mixed-script and code-switched inputs
  • Domain-specific quality and customer-support language

Responsible use and limitations

Generated translations can be inaccurate, incomplete, culturally inappropriate, or contextually misleading. The confidence proxy is not a guarantee of correctness. Do not enter private, confidential, personal, copyrighted, legal, medical, immigration, safety-critical, or business-critical content. Human review is required before real-world use.
