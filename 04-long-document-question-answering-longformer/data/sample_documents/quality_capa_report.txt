QUALITY CORRECTIVE AND PREVENTIVE ACTION REPORT
Document ID: CAPA-DEMO-2026-004
Status: Closed after effectiveness review
This is a synthetic portfolio document and does not contain company data.

1. Executive Summary
A quality review identified intermittent discoloration on labels applied to
Total Kjeldahl Nitrogen reagent containers. The issue was limited to a small
demonstration lot and did not affect reagent chemistry or analytical
performance. Distribution was paused while the investigation was completed.

2. Problem Statement
During incoming inspection, operators observed that several labels had a faded
blue region after routine handling. The complaint history showed similar visual
observations but no barcode-readability failures. The investigation team
therefore treated the event as an appearance and supplier-process issue.

3. Investigation
The team reviewed printing records, environmental logs, adhesive certificates,
incoming inspection images, and supplier batch documentation. Barcode scanners
successfully read every tested label. Accelerated light exposure reproduced the
discoloration only on labels made during one supplier curing cycle.

4. Root Cause
The confirmed root cause was variation in the supplier's ultraviolet curing
time. A maintenance adjustment had reduced lamp intensity, but the supplier's
work instruction did not require an immediate verification check after that
adjustment.

5. Corrective Action
Priya Raman was assigned as the CAPA owner. The supplier restored the validated
curing settings, added an hourly intensity verification, and revised the work
instruction. Incoming inspection added a temporary color-density check for the
next five received lots.

6. Preventive Action
The approved preventive action was to add curing-intensity trend review to the
quarterly supplier scorecard. Supplier Quality Engineering will review the
trend, while Site Quality will verify that the incoming inspection control is
removed only after successful effectiveness evidence.

7. Effectiveness Check
Five consecutive lots passed visual, barcode, and color-density inspection.
No related complaint was observed during the 90-day monitoring window. The
effectiveness review was approved on June 18, 2026.

8. Limitations
This synthetic report is intended only for demonstrating document question
answering, evidence localization, and portfolio deployment.
