SUPPLIER QUALITY REVIEW — SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION

Scope
This review covers a fictional supplier of molded optical housings used in a
water-quality instrument. It is safe sample content created for this portfolio.

Performance Summary
On-time delivery improved from 91 percent to 97 percent during the review
period. Incoming inspection acceptance remained above 99 percent. Two minor
nonconformances involved cosmetic surface marks and were contained before
production use.

Audit Finding
The audit found that calibration labels on one measurement fixture were
difficult to read. The calibration itself was current and traceable. The
supplier replaced the labels on the day of the audit and added a legibility
check to the weekly layered process audit.

Risk Assessment
The finding was rated low risk because measurement status was independently
verified in the electronic calibration system. No affected product escaped,
and no measurement result required correction.

Ownership and Due Date
Elena Martinez owns the supplier follow-up plan. Evidence of the revised layered
audit was due by August 15, 2026. Site Supplier Quality Engineering was
responsible for verifying closure.

Closure Evidence
The supplier submitted three completed weekly audit records, photographs of the
new labels, and the updated audit checklist. The closure package was accepted
after document review.
